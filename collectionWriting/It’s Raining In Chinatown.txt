It’s Raining In Chinatown

It’s Raining In Chinatown is a collection born of immediacy. On a rainy afternoon in Chinatown, I saw the unapologetic presence of cultural artifacts—Buddhas in warehouses, utility and reverence side by side. This series is a visceral entry point—“This is crazy!”—but for those who look deeper, it reveals layers of cultural continuity, personal discovery, and the beauty of the present moment.”
